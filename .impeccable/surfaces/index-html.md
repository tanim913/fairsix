# Surface: Fairsix app (index.html)

Scope: the whole single-page app: home (create or join), lobby, game board, result, proof sheet, rules sheet. Mode: operate.

Audience and job: friends and family on phones starting a private Ludo game in seconds and playing it to the end, sure the dice are fair.

Constraints: phone portrait first (320px up), tablet and desktop use the space beside the board for seats and the roll log. Touch targets 44px. Light and dark themes.

User steer (binding): Kancha Glass, with every edge rounded (no sharp corners anywhere, board squares are rounded tiles), stronger contrast than the sketch, and cuter guti (tokens).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every guti is a kancha glass marble and every roll is each player's sealed bead melting into one die. Refuses the glossy gold-framed 3D board on a purple gradient that commercial Ludo apps ship.

OWN-WORLD: Clear-glass ground (light: #E4EEEA mint-frosted; dark: bottle-glass #0C1A1E) with ink #0F1B33. Four marble colours at full saturation: ruby #D0263F, bottle green #12804F, amber #E39A00, cobalt #1F45D6. Rounded tiles with 2px gaps, pill buttons, every corner rounded. Marbles: opaque coloured core, clear rim, a big soft highlight, two tiny eyes that blink. One reserved colour, violet #7B2FF7, marks only legal landing squares and movable guti (ring + soft pulse). Captured guti show an x_x face while they fly home. Type: Gabarito.

STORY: Players see whose turn it is, roll, tap a glowing guti, watch it hop. Under the die a live line counts sealed seeds; the Proof sheet shows every roll's number, face and contributing beads, recomputable.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: top bar (brand bead, room code pill, menu); seat chips row (4 marbles with names, active one ringed + timer bar); board full width; bottom tray: glass die left, big Roll pill right; seed line below with Proof link.

FORM: candidate 7 of 7 (glass marbles and bangles), seed:a37939a8d4bcf27c. Raises: whole-cell sizing (Crouwel), one reserved live colour (orienteering), numbered rolls key their proof (doujin), one bead form at every scale (Emigre), live seed readout (variable specimen). Signature move: seal-and-mix: on each roll the contributing players' beads slide into the die tray and the die resolves.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
