            How to play

            Classic

            • Roll a 6 to bring a piece out of your base.
            • Roll first, move after: a 6 means you roll again straight away, and your dice stack up (for example 6 + 6 + 3). When you have nothing left to roll, pick a die, then tap a piece, until all your dice are used.
            • A third 6 in a row cancels your turn, and none of those dice are used.
            • Land on a lone opponent piece to send it back to base. That earns another roll, which you take before using the rest of your dice.
            • Start squares and star squares are safe. Two pieces of one colour on a square protect each other, but anyone can pass or share the square.
            • Go round the board and up your coloured lane. You need the exact number to reach home, and reaching home earns another roll.
            • With two players, you sit on opposite corners: bottom-left and top-right.
            • The first player with all four pieces home wins. Everyone else plays on for the other places.

            Arrow

            • All the Classic rules, plus one arrow for each colour, drawn in that colour.
            • Your arrow starts two squares before your home lane. Land exactly on its tail to fly straight into your home lane and earn another roll. Only your own colour's arrow works for you.
            • Passing over the tail does nothing. Other colours' arrows are ordinary squares for you.
            • The four short arrows at the centre corners work for every colour: land exactly on one's tail to hop one square on and earn another roll.
            • First with all four pieces home wins.

            Re-rolls

            • The host sets how many re-rolls each player gets: off, 1, 3 or 5. Everyone gets the same number, and nobody can earn or buy more.
            • Right after you roll, tap Re-roll to throw away that die and roll it again. A re-roll is a new sealed roll, exactly as fair as any other.
            • With one possible move or none, your roll is kept after 3 seconds. Rolling again or moving a piece also keeps it.
            • Re-rolled throws still show in the proof, marked as re-rolled.

            Turns

            • You have 30 seconds to roll and 30 to move. If time runs out, auto-play takes over on your phone: it rolls with your own sealed dice and plays sensible moves until you tap I'm back (or roll or move yourself).
            • If your phone itself goes quiet (closed or offline), your turns are skipped. Three skipped turns in a row and you are out.
            • Against the computer there is no clock: take your time.

            Playing the computer

            The computer plays by the same rules with the same number of re-rolls. Its dice are sealed exactly like a person's: it adds its own secret to your rolls and you add yours to its rolls, so it can't know or steer a roll before it lands. It decides only from what is on the board. Easy often picks any legal move and only re-rolls a third 6. Sharp weighs every move: captures, getting pieces home, arrows, staying out of reach, pairing up for safety, and saves its re-rolls for wasted rolls. Leave mid-game and you can carry on later from the home screen.

            Why the dice are fair

            Nothing about you (your history, your wins, your device, your chosen die) goes into a roll. Each roll needs a secret from you and from the other players, all sealed before the first roll. If a phone goes quiet, the roll waits 4 seconds and then goes ahead with the rest. Open Proof during a game to recheck every roll.
